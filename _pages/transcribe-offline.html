Proof of concept: speech-to-text that runs entirely on this device using Whisper in the browser. The first run downloads the model (~40–80 MB) and caches it; after that, recording and transcription work with no internet at all.

Tap “Load model” to begin. On an iPad this needs Safari 16.4 or newer.

Notes for the iPad: keep this tab in the foreground while recording (Safari pauses background tabs). Audio files exported from a recorder app (e.g. VIAIM Note → share to Files) can be picked with the file button. Everything stays on the device — no audio or text leaves it.
