Live in-person meeting transcription — runs entirely in your browser using its built-in speech recognition. Nothing is stored on a server; your session autosaves locally, and you can export a Markdown note for this garden when you're done.

⚠️ This browser doesn't support the Web Speech API. Try Chrome, Edge, or Safari. You can still type notes below and export them.
